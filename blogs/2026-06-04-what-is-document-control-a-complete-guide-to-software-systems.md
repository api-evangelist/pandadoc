This guide explains document control as the discipline of managing document creation, review, approval, distribution, and storage to ensure integrity and compliance. Unlike document management, which prioritizes accessibility, document control emphasizes version accuracy and regulatory requirements. The article covers essential software capabilities including version tracking, approval routing, access controls, and audit trails, along with industry-specific applications and best practices for implementation.
