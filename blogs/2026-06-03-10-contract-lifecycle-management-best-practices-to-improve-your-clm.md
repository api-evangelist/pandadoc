This article outlines ten contract lifecycle management best practices organized across three stages: pre-signature (centralized intake, templates, AI drafting, approval workflows, redlining), execution (electronic signatures), and post-signature (repository storage, obligation tracking, data extraction, KPI measurement). The practices aim to help SMBs and mid-market companies reduce missed renewals, approval delays, and legal risks through structured, repeatable processes rather than informal email-based contract management.
