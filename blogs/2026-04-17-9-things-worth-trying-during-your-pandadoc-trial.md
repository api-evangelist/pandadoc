<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Upload a PDF and DOCX file to see how PandaDoc handles each differently</h2>



<p>With PandaDoc, you don’t have to recreate your entire document library from scratch. Instead, you <a href="https://developers.pandadoc.com/reference/create-document-from-upload">can import documents that you already use</a> by uploading them to your PandaDoc workspace.</p>



<p>Try importing both a PDF and a DOCX file to get a better look at how PandaDoc handles both documents.</p>



<p></p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>PDFs</strong> are locked, and edits are limited. The document will retain its original format, but you won’t be able to make major changes. In this scenario, you’ll be limited to using <a href="https://support.pandadoc.com/en/articles/9714583-fields">PandaDoc Fields</a> to modify the document.</li>



<li><strong>DOCX</strong> files are fully editable, meaning that you can update the text, adjust sections, add new content blocks, and modify the content as if the document were created natively in the editor. DOCX files can even be <a href="https://support.pandadoc.com/en/articles/9714561-convert-docx-files-into-editable-templates-documents">converted to and saved as templates</a>.</li>
</ul>



<p>This side-by-side comparison will give you a better understanding of how PandaDoc handles different document types. As a general rule, only DOCX and native PandaDoc files are fully editable. All other documents are restricted to drag-and-drop fields and minor modifications.</p>

<p></p>



<p>For a full list of file types that you can import to PandaDoc, review <a href="https://support.pandadoc.com/en/articles/11197006-how-to-upload-a-document">this support article</a>.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">3. Use variables to standardize a document</h2>



<p>One of the easiest ways to improve your documents is to standardize how information is used and distributed.</p>



<p><a href="https://support.pandadoc.com/en/articles/9714599-variables">Variables</a> allow you to define key pieces of information, such as a client or company name, and apply them throughout your entire document. Rather than editing those same details over and over, you can simply update the variable and let PandaDoc take care of the rest.</p>



<p></p>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img alt="" class="wp-image-67921" src="https://public-site.marketing.pandadoc-static.com/app/uploads/sites/3/Screenshot-2026-04-13-at-9.00.04%E2%80%AFAM-1024x643.jpg" /></figure>



<p>For example, a proposal carrying variables such as <strong>[Company.Name]</strong> and <strong>[Customer.Name]</strong> can become the standard document that your organization uses to submit bids. When a proposal needs to be sent, a sales rep can create a new document from an existing template, change the variable values once in the document settings, and send the document.</p>



<p>For advanced users, it’s also possible to <a href="https://www.pandadoc.com/integrations/crm/">connect your CRM</a> to PandaDoc. When configured correctly, PandaDoc will replace variables with data pulled directly from the CRM, eliminating the need for manual inputs during document preparation.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">5. Send a document to yourself and experience signing it like a client</h2>



<p>When you send a document for signature with PandaDoc, recipients follow <a href="https://support.pandadoc.com/en/articles/9714786-sign-a-pandadoc-recipient-s-guide">a streamlined workflow</a> focused on reviewing and signing rather than document creation. While there’s no need to create an account or download any software, the interface is slightly different from what you’ll experience as a PandaDoc user.</p>



<p>As part of your trial, we recommend taking a few minutes to send a document to yourself and experience the signing process from the receiving end.</p>



<p>To do this, create a document and <span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px;">set the recipient to a second email addr</span>ess you control. Once the document is prepared, send it to yourself, click the link in the email, and follow the provided instructions.</p>



<p>By going through this process, you can confirm that documents are easy to access, simple to complete, and ready for use with real clients. It’s a practical way to validate the experience, and it sets you up to answer any questions that a signer might have during review.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">6. Open the audit trail to see everything your document tracks behind the scenes</h2>



<p></p>



<p></p>



<p>While PandaDoc doesn’t require users to create an account, our software captures several critical details during the document creation and signing process to verify signer identity.</p>



<p>The bulk of the information we collect ends up in the <a href="https://www.pandadoc.com/ask/what-is-an-audit-trail/">audit trail</a> attached to a document. By reviewing the audit trail, you’ll see <a href="https://support.pandadoc.com/en/articles/9714819-review-audit-trail-for-sent-documents">a detailed timeline</a> of every document interaction, including when the document was sent, opened, viewed, and signed. Edits and document changes are also attached, providing a complete record of document activity from creation to finalization.</p>



<p><strong>Security note: </strong>PandaDoc verifies signer identity through unique signing links sent via email to specific recipients. One unique link is generated for each user.<br />When sending a document to signers, be sure to send it to an unique email address that they control so that access is limited and the chain of custody isn’t compromised.</p>



<p>After the signing process is complete, <a href="https://support.pandadoc.com/en/articles/9714781-signature-certificate-of-completed-documents">a unique signing certificate</a> can be downloaded along with a copy of the final document. The certificate contains IP addresses, a document reference number, timestamps, and more to protect against repudiation and confirm signer identity.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">7. Set up an approval workflow and eliminate back-and-forth before sending</h2>



<p></p>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img alt="" class="wp-image-67945" height="639" src="https://public-site.marketing.pandadoc-static.com/app/uploads/sites/3/2025-01-31_17-09-07-1024x639.png" width="1024" /></figure>



<p></p>



<p>If your documents regularly require a second set of eyes, <a href="https://support.pandadoc.com/en/articles/9714799-approval-workflow">approval workflows</a> can simplify that process.</p>



<p>In PandaDoc, <span style="margin: 0px; padding: 0px;">you can <a href="https://learn.pandadoc.com/setting-up-approval-workflows" target="_blank">set up simple approval steps</a> so documents are automatically reviewed</span> before they’re sent to customers. Once the workflow is set up, PandaDoc automatically handles routing and notifications, but the document is held internally until the approval process is complete.</p>



<p>Approval workflows are meant to replace more informal approval processes, such as chat or email, with something more systematic and structured. By taking this approach, you can reduce the likelihood of errors and ensure that every document is correct before it reaches the customer.</p>



<p>At the enterprise level, you can also create <a href="https://support.pandadoc.com/en/articles/9714721-conditional-approvals-with-quote-builder">rules-based approval thresholds</a> for proposals and quotes with PandaDoc CPQ. For example, a quote that exceeds a set dollar threshold might trigger a review, while quotes that fall below the threshold are sent automatically. While you won’t see this functionality in the standard free trial, it’s worth noting that PandaDoc’s approval capability can become far more dynamic if needed.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">9. Build a pricing table that calculates totals automatically</h2>



<p></p>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img alt="" class="wp-image-67955" src="https://public-site.marketing.pandadoc-static.com/app/uploads/sites/3/Screenshot-2026-04-13-at-9.27.44%E2%80%AFAM-1024x664.jpg" /></figure>



<p></p>



<p>If you’re using PandaDoc to create quotes, proposals, or other documents with pricing, try adding a <a href="https://www.pandadoc.com/features/quote/pricing-table/">pricing table</a>.</p>



<p>Pricing tables can be inserted as standalone modules inside any PandaDoc document. Once added, you’ll be able to build out line items, adjust quantities, and apply discounts directly inside the document. As you make changes, PandaDoc will automatically calculate and adjust totals, so you don’t have to worry about formulas or manual updates.</p>



<p>If you sell materials, products, or quantifiable services, you can also connect your pricing table to your workspace’s <a href="https://www.pandadoc.com/features/quote/product-catalog/">Product Catalog</a>. After products are added to the catalog, they’ll auto-populate as users start adding line items to the pricing table.</p>



<p>It’s even possible to pre-load items in a pricing table by adding them to a document before saving it as a template. Each time the template is loaded, any pre-set line items will populate automatically.</p>
